## Get Up and Running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.firearmoryvault.com](https://app.firearmoryvault.com) and verify your email address. Your account is protected with end-to-end encryption from day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first firearm">
    Tap **Add Firearm** and enter the name, model, and manufacturer. The AI engine automatically organizes the entry in your collection database.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload schematics and documents">
    Attach firearm schematics, background checks, and purchase receipts directly to each entry for complete, searchable records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the Caliber Wiki">
    Browse the built-in wiki for instant ballistic data, common uses, and case images for hundreds of calibers.
  </Step>
</Steps>
