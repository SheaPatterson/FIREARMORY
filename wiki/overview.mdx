The FireArmory Vault caliber wiki is a fully integrated reference tool built directly into the app — no external browser tabs or third-party lookups required. Whether you're cataloging a new acquisition, comparing cartridges for a potential purchase, or simply brushing up on a caliber you don't shoot every day, the wiki puts authoritative data at your fingertips right alongside your collection.

## What's in the wiki

Every caliber entry in the wiki covers the details that matter most to collectors and shooters. Use the cards below to get a sense of what you'll find on any given caliber page.

## Browsing the wiki

Access the caliber wiki from anywhere in the app using one of two methods:

- **Bottom navigation bar** — Tap the **Wiki** tab at the bottom of the screen to open the wiki home screen directly.
- **Firearm entry caliber field** — Open any firearm in your collection and tap its caliber field to jump straight to that caliber's dedicated wiki page.

Once inside the wiki, you can navigate in two ways:

- **Browse by caliber family** — Calibers are organized into families (handgun, rifle, rimfire, shotgun, and more) so you can explore related cartridges together.
- **Search by name** — Use the search bar at the top of the wiki screen to find any caliber by its common name, designation, or metric equivalent (for example, search "9mm," "Luger," or "9×19").

## Linking wiki entries to your collection

Every firearm entry in your Vault collection includes a caliber field that is directly connected to the wiki. When you assign a caliber to a firearm during entry or editing, that field becomes a live link — tap it at any time to open the full wiki page for that cartridge. This means you never have to leave your collection view to look up specs, compare loads, or check case dimensions.

<Tip>
  Tap the caliber field on any firearm entry to jump directly to that caliber's wiki page.
</Tip>
