FireArmory Vault gives you a dedicated, secure home for every technical document tied to your collection. Whether you're referencing a manufacturer's assembly diagram during a cleaning session or archiving a custom build blueprint for long-term record-keeping, the Schematics feature keeps those documents organized, encrypted, and always within reach alongside the firearm they belong to.

## What you can store

Attach any of the following document types directly to a firearm entry in your collection:

- **Manufacturer assembly diagrams** — official exploded-view documents published by the firearm's maker, showing how every component fits together
- **Parts breakdowns** — detailed lists and illustrations identifying individual parts, their positions, and their relationships within the assembly
- **Custom build blueprints** — technical drawings or design files for custom or home-built firearms, preserving the exact specifications of your build
- **Exploded view diagrams** — high-detail renderings that separate each component visually, making them ideal for field-stripping reference and maintenance

## Supported file types

FireArmory Vault accepts the following file formats for schematic uploads:

- **PDF** — ideal for multi-page assembly manuals and technical documents
- **PNG** — lossless image format suited for diagrams with sharp lines and text
- **JPG** — suitable for scanned documents and photographic reference images
- **SVG** — vector format that scales without quality loss, recommended for precision technical drawings

For the best viewing experience in the built-in viewer, upload images at a minimum resolution of **300 DPI**. Higher-resolution files render more clearly when zoomed in on fine part labels and measurements.

<Note>
  Schematics are linked to individual firearm entries in your collection. To upload a schematic, you must first create the corresponding firearm entry.
</Note>
