Attaching schematics directly to a firearm entry means your assembly diagrams, parts breakdowns, and build blueprints are always in context — right next to the specs, photos, and maintenance history for that specific firearm. The upload process is straightforward: select the firearm entry, tap into the Schematics tab, and add your file in a few taps.

## Before you upload

Make sure the following are in place before starting an upload:

- **A firearm entry exists** — schematics must be attached to an existing entry in your collection. If you haven't created the entry yet, go to your collection and add the firearm first.
- **A supported file format** — your file must be a PDF, PNG, JPG, or SVG. Other formats are not accepted by the Vault uploader.
- **File size within the limit** — each individual schematic upload has a maximum file size. For large high-resolution PDFs, compress the file before uploading to stay within the limit.

## Uploading a schematic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the firearm entry">
    Navigate to `app.firearmoryvault.com` and open the firearm entry in your collection that you want to attach the schematic to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the 'Schematics' tab">
    Inside the firearm entry, tap the **Schematics** tab to open the schematic management panel for that entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Upload Schematic'">
    Tap the **Upload Schematic** button to launch the file picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your file from device storage">
    Browse your device storage and select the schematic file you want to upload. The uploader accepts PDF, PNG, JPG, and SVG files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a descriptive label">
    Enter a label for the schematic — for example, **Factory Assembly Diagram** or **Trigger Group Exploded View**. A clear label makes the file easier to identify and search later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Save'">
    Tap **Save** to upload and attach the schematic to the firearm entry. The file is encrypted and stored in your Vault immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use descriptive labels when uploading — for example, **Glock 19 Gen5 Exploded View** is far more searchable than **scan1.pdf**. Good labels save time when you're looking for a specific document across a large collection.
</Tip>

## Uploading building blueprints

Building blueprints follow the same upload process as firearm schematics. Select your blueprint file, add a label, and tap **Save** to store it securely in your Vault.

In addition to attaching blueprints at the individual firearm level, you can also store blueprints at the **collection level** — useful for range layouts, storage facility plans, or any blueprint that isn't tied to a single firearm. To upload a collection-level blueprint, navigate to your collection's main settings page and look for the **Blueprints** section.

## Managing uploaded schematics

Once a schematic is uploaded, you can manage it directly from the **Schematics** tab of the firearm entry:

- **Rename** — tap the schematic label to edit it and save a new name
- **Download** — tap the download icon to save the original file back to your device
- **Delete** — tap the delete icon to permanently remove the schematic from your Vault

<Warning>
  Deleting a schematic is permanent and cannot be undone. The file is removed from your Vault and cannot be recovered. Download a copy before deleting if you want to keep the file.
</Warning>
