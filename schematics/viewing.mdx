FireArmory Vault includes a built-in schematic viewer that lets you inspect assembly diagrams, parts breakdowns, and blueprints without leaving the app. The viewer is optimized for both mobile and desktop, with smooth zoom and pan on any screen size. You can drill into fine part labels, torque specs, or assembly tolerances. Because everything renders inside the Vault, your files never leave the encrypted environment to be opened in an external application.

## Opening a schematic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the firearm entry">
    Open `app.firearmoryvault.com` and go to the collection entry for the firearm whose schematics you want to view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the 'Schematics' tab">
    Inside the firearm entry, tap the **Schematics** tab to see all schematics attached to that entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the schematic name to open the viewer">
    Tap the name or thumbnail of the schematic you want to inspect. The built-in viewer opens the file immediately, with all controls available in the toolbar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewer controls

The built-in viewer provides the following navigation controls:

- **Zoom in / zoom out**
  - *Mobile:* Use a two-finger pinch-to-zoom gesture to zoom in or out on any part of the schematic.
  - *Desktop:* Scroll up with your mouse wheel or trackpad to zoom in, and scroll down to zoom out. You can also use the **+** and **−** buttons in the viewer toolbar.
- **Pan** — tap and drag (mobile) or click and drag (desktop) to move around the schematic after zooming in. This lets you scan across wide assembly diagrams without losing your zoom level.
- **Full-screen mode** — tap the full-screen icon in the viewer toolbar to expand the schematic to fill your entire display. Press **Esc** or tap the icon again to exit full-screen.
- **Page navigation** — for multi-page PDF schematics, use the **previous page** (‹) and **next page** (›) arrows in the toolbar, or enter a specific page number directly to jump to it.

## Downloading a schematic

To save a copy of a schematic from the viewer to your device:

1. While the schematic is open in the viewer, tap the **Download** icon in the toolbar.
2. Choose a save location on your device when prompted.

The viewer downloads the original file exactly as it was uploaded — no quality is lost in the process.

## Sharing schematics

Schematics stored in your Vault are **private by default**. They are accessible only to you when signed in to your FireArmory Vault account. There is no option to generate a shareable external link or export a schematic to a third-party service. This privacy model ensures your technical documents and build blueprints remain under your exclusive control at all times.

If you need to share a schematic with another person, download the original file from the viewer and send it through your preferred channel outside of the Vault.

<Info>
  The viewer renders schematics directly in the app — no third-party app required. For best results on mobile, use landscape orientation when viewing wide assembly diagrams so the full width of the schematic fits on screen.
</Info>
