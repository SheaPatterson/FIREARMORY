This guide walks you through creating your FireArmory Vault account and configuring it for day-to-day use. You will cover email verification, two-factor authentication, profile preferences, and subscription options — everything you need before you start adding firearms and uploading documents.

## Creating your account

Follow these steps to register and sign in to FireArmory Vault for the first time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the app">
    Open a browser and go to [app.firearmoryvault.com](https://app.firearmoryvault.com). Tap **Sign Up** on the landing page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Provide your **email address** and choose a strong **password** of at least 12 characters. FireArmory Vault enforces password strength requirements to protect your sensitive data. Tap **Create Account** to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from FireArmory Vault. Tap the **Verify Email** link inside. The link expires after 24 hours. If it expires, request a new one from the sign-in screen.

    <Note>
      You must verify your email address before you can upload documents or schematics. Other features, including adding firearms and browsing the Caliber Wiki, are available immediately after registration.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Return to [app.firearmoryvault.com](https://app.firearmoryvault.com), enter your email and password, and tap **Sign In**. You are now inside your Vault.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Your Vault holds serial numbers, purchase records, compliance documents, and sensitive schematics. Two-factor authentication adds a second layer of protection so that a compromised password alone is not enough for an attacker to access your data. We strongly recommend enabling 2FA before you store any sensitive information.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security settings">
    Tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner and select **Settings**, then navigate to the **Security** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable two-factor authentication">
    Tap **Enable 2FA**. FireArmory Vault supports time-based one-time password (TOTP) apps such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or any compatible authenticator. Scan the QR code displayed on screen with your authenticator app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the setup">
    Enter the six-digit code currently shown in your authenticator app and tap **Confirm**. FireArmory Vault verifies the code to ensure your app is synced correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery codes">
    After confirmation, FireArmory Vault displays a set of one-time recovery codes. Download or copy these codes and store them somewhere safe — such as a password manager or printed in a secure location. Each code can be used once to regain access if you lose your 2FA device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Store your recovery codes safely. If you lose access to your 2FA device and do not have your recovery codes, regaining access to your account requires identity verification through FireArmory Vault's support team, which may take additional time. Recovery codes cannot be regenerated after initial setup without first disabling and re-enabling 2FA.
</Warning>

## Profile settings

Personalize your account to match how you work.

Navigate to **Settings → Profile** to update:

- **Display name** — choose a name that appears throughout the app and on any exports or reports you generate.
- **Notification preferences** — configure whether you receive email notifications for events such as document upload confirmations, new wiki entries, or account security alerts. Toggle individual notification categories on or off to keep your inbox focused.

Tap **Save Changes** after updating any profile field.

## Subscription & billing

FireArmory Vault offers both free and paid plan tiers to suit different needs.

- **Free tier** — includes core collection management, basic record tracking, and read-only access to the Caliber Wiki, with a limit on the number of stored documents.
- **Paid plans** — unlock unlimited document and schematic storage, advanced AI organization features, priority support, and full Caliber Wiki access including ballistic comparison tools.

To view your current plan or upgrade, navigate to **Settings → Subscription**. From there you can review what your current tier includes, compare available plans, and enter billing information to upgrade. Changes take effect immediately upon successful payment.
