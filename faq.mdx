## Account & Privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is my data private? Who can see my collection?">
    FireArmory Vault uses end-to-end encryption, which means your collection data is encrypted on your device before it ever leaves it. Only you — authenticated with your credentials — can decrypt and view your collection. No FireArmory Vault employee, support agent, or third party can access the contents of your collection, your documents, or your schematics.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel my subscription?">
    When you cancel your subscription, your account enters a 30-day grace period during which you can still log in, export your data, and download any stored documents or schematics. After the grace period ends, your account and all associated data are permanently deleted from our servers. We strongly recommend exporting your collection before your grace period expires.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use FireArmory Vault without an internet connection?">
    FireArmory Vault supports offline access for viewing collection entries that have already synced to your device. You can browse your firearms list, review stored details, and consult the caliber wiki while offline. However, uploading new documents, adding new entries, and triggering AI analysis all require an active internet connection. Your changes will sync automatically the next time your device comes online.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    To permanently delete your account, navigate to **Settings → Account → Delete Account** in the app at [app.firearmoryvault.com](https://app.firearmoryvault.com). You will be asked to confirm your password and acknowledge that all data will be permanently erased. Deletion is irreversible — export your collection data before proceeding. If you are unable to access your account to initiate deletion, contact [support@firearmoryvault.com](mailto:support@firearmoryvault.com) for assistance.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## AI Features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does the AI organize my collection?">
    When you add a new firearm, FireArmory Vault's AI analyzes the name, model, and manufacturer information you provide and automatically categorizes the entry within your collection. It assigns relevant tags, groups related firearms by platform or caliber family, and fills in supplementary metadata — such as action type and country of origin — where it can confidently do so. The goal is to reduce manual data entry while keeping your collection consistently organized.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if the AI doesn't recognize a firearm I'm adding?">
    If the AI cannot identify a firearm from the information provided, it will flag the entry as unrecognized and leave the auto-populated fields blank rather than guessing incorrectly. You can fill in all fields manually and save the entry normally. To help improve future recognition, you can also submit feedback directly from the entry screen using the **Report unrecognized firearm** option, which sends the details to our data team for review.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I override the AI's suggestions?">
    Yes — every field the AI populates is fully editable. Open any collection entry, tap **Edit**, and modify any field you disagree with. Your manual changes are always preserved; re-running AI analysis on an entry will not overwrite fields you have edited manually unless you explicitly choose to reset them. You are always in full control of your collection data.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Collection Management

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many firearms can I store in my collection?">
    The number of entries you can store depends on your subscription tier. The standard plan supports up to 250 firearm entries, while the Pro plan offers unlimited entries. Each entry can include serial number, purchase date, estimated value, associated documents, background check records, receipts, and schematics. You can view and upgrade your current plan at any time under **Settings → Subscription**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What document file types are supported for uploads?">
    FireArmory Vault accepts **PDF**, **PNG**, and **JPG** files for document uploads, including background check records, purchase receipts, and general supporting paperwork. For best results, scan documents at a minimum of 300 DPI so that text remains legible when you review them later. Files must be under the per-file size limit; see the schematic section below for size details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export my collection data?">
    Yes. Navigate to **Settings → Export Collection** to download your full collection as a structured **CSV** file, which includes all entry fields such as firearm name, model, manufacturer, serial number, purchase date, and estimated value. Attached documents and schematics can be downloaded individually from each entry. We recommend performing a full export periodically as a personal backup.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Schematics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What schematic file formats are supported?">
    FireArmory Vault accepts schematics uploaded as **PDF**, **PNG**, or **JPG** files. PDF is recommended for multi-page technical drawings because it preserves vector quality and allows you to zoom in without loss of detail. PNG is the preferred format for single-page high-resolution raster schematics. JPG is supported but is a lossy format, so fine line detail may degrade at high compression levels.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a file size limit for schematics?">
    Each individual schematic or document file must be **25 MB or smaller**. If your file exceeds this limit, compress the PDF using a lossless compression tool, or reduce the image resolution before re-uploading. There is no limit on the total number of schematic files you can attach to a single collection entry, but each file must individually fall within the size limit.

    <Tip>
      For large multi-page PDFs, splitting the document into sections and uploading them as separate files is a reliable way to stay within the per-file size limit without sacrificing image quality.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Caliber Wiki

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How often is the caliber wiki updated?">
    The built-in caliber wiki is updated on a rolling basis as our data team verifies and adds new entries. Major caliber families — including common pistol, rifle, and shotgun cartridges — are already covered with ballistic data, physical characteristics, and case imagery. Niche, obsolete, or newly introduced cartridges are added as the team completes verification. You can check the **Wiki** section in the app to browse available entries.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I request a caliber to be added to the wiki?">
    Absolutely. Open the **Caliber Wiki** section in the app and tap **Request a Caliber** at the bottom of the page. Fill in the cartridge name and any reference information you have, and submit the request. Our data team reviews all submissions and prioritizes entries based on request volume. You will receive an in-app notification when a caliber you requested goes live in the wiki.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
