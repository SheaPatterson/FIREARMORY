FireArmory Vault is the first AI-powered platform built specifically for firearms collectors and professionals. Whether you maintain a handful of heirlooms or manage a large professional inventory, Vault gives you a single secure place to organize your collection, store critical documents, track values, and explore detailed ballistic data — all protected by end-to-end encryption.

## What is FireArmory Vault?

FireArmory Vault brings together every tool a firearms owner needs into one intelligent platform. Its AI engine automatically classifies and organizes your collection the moment you enter a firearm, while built-in document storage, record tracking, and a comprehensive caliber wiki keep everything you need within reach.

## Who Is It For?

FireArmory Vault is designed for anyone who takes their collection seriously.

**Enthusiasts and hobbyists** use Vault to keep their personal collections organized, track what they paid for each firearm, store documentation for insurance purposes, and look up caliber specifications without leaving the app.

**Professionals — including dealers, gunsmiths, and range operators** — use Vault to maintain accurate inventory records, store compliance documents such as background checks and transfer receipts, and manage schematics for service and repair work. The AI-powered organization layer means less manual data entry and fewer filing errors, even across large inventories.

## How It Works

Getting up and running with FireArmory Vault takes only a few minutes. Here is the high-level workflow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.firearmoryvault.com](https://app.firearmoryvault.com). Verify your email address to unlock full access to document storage and schematics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your firearms">
    Tap **Add Firearm** and enter the name, model, and manufacturer. The AI engine instantly organizes the entry and places it in the right category within your collection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your documents">
    Attach receipts, background check records, blueprints, or schematics directly to each firearm entry. All uploads are encrypted automatically on the way in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the Caliber Wiki">
    Search the built-in wiki for any caliber. Browse ballistic data, case dimensions, and characteristics to deepen your knowledge or quickly answer a question in the field.
  </Step>
</Steps>
