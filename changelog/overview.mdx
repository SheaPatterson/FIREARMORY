<Update label="August 22, 2026" description="Initial release">

FireArmory Vault is live. This first release brings the full collection management platform to enthusiasts and professionals, with AI-powered organization, encrypted document storage, and a built-in caliber wiki.

## New features

- **AI-powered collection organization.** Add a firearm by name, model, and manufacturer, and the AI engine automatically categorizes and sorts the entry into your collection. See [Adding firearms](/collection/adding-firearms) and [AI organization](/collection/ai-organization).
- **Schematics and blueprints.** Upload building blueprints and firearm schematics and attach them directly to any firearm entry for instant retrieval. See [Uploading schematics](/schematics/uploading) and [Viewing schematics](/schematics/viewing).
- **Serial number and value tracking.** Record serial numbers, purchase dates, and estimated values for every firearm to keep a complete ownership history for insurance, resale, or compliance. See [Serial numbers](/collection/serial-numbers).
- **Document storage.** Store background checks, purchase receipts, and transfer paperwork alongside the relevant firearm. See [Documents](/collection/documents).
- **Caliber wiki.** Browse a built-in reference library covering ballistic data, case images, and physical characteristics across a wide range of calibers. See [Ballistic data](/wiki/ballistic-data) and [Caliber comparison](/wiki/caliber-comparison).
- **End-to-end encryption.** Every record, document, and schematic in your Vault is encrypted end-to-end. Only you hold the keys. See [Encryption](/security/encryption) and [Data storage](/security/data-storage).
- **Account security controls.** Manage sign-in, verification, and recovery options from a single place. See [Account security](/security/account-security).

## Getting started

New to FireArmory Vault? Start with the [Quickstart](/quickstart) to create your account, add your first firearm, and upload your first document in a few minutes.
