Automatic AI organization is what sets FireArmory Vault apart from a spreadsheet or a generic inventory app. The moment you enter a firearm's name, model, and manufacturer, the AI engine goes to work — matching your entry against a comprehensive firearms database, populating metadata you would otherwise have to look up and type by hand, and keeping your entire collection consistently categorized. The result is a collection that stays organized as it grows, with minimal effort on your part.

## How it works

When you create or edit a firearm entry, FireArmory Vault's AI analyzes the three core fields — **Name**, **Model**, and **Manufacturer** — and cross-references them against its firearms knowledge base. If the AI finds a confident match, it pre-fills a set of suggested fields and presents them for your review before you save.

The process happens automatically in the background while you're still filling out the form. By the time you finish entering the required fields, the AI suggestions are ready for you to review.

The AI does not modify your entry without your review. Every suggestion is presented as a draft that you can accept as-is, edit, or discard entirely before saving.

## AI-suggested fields

Depending on the firearm and the confidence of the match, the AI may suggest values for the following fields:

- **Manufacturer details** — full legal name, country of origin, and founding year for the manufacturer
- **Action type classification** — the operating mechanism (e.g., Semi-Automatic Pistol, Bolt-Action Rifle, Double-Action Revolver)
- **Caliber family grouping** — the cartridge family the firearm belongs to, used to group entries in filters
- **Related models** — other models in the same product line or generation, surfaced as informational context

<Note>
  The AI works best with complete manufacturer names. For example, use **Smith & Wesson** rather than **S&W** for the most accurate suggestions. Abbreviated or informal names may reduce match confidence.
</Note>

## Accepting and editing suggestions

After you fill in the required fields and the AI analysis completes, a **Review AI Suggestions** panel appears below the entry form. Each suggested field is shown with the AI's proposed value and a confidence indicator.

To work with the suggestions:

- **Accept a suggestion** — tap the checkmark next to the field to accept the AI's value as-is.
- **Edit a suggestion** — tap the field value to open it for manual editing. Type your preferred value and confirm.
- **Dismiss a suggestion** — tap the **×** next to any field to clear the AI's suggestion and leave that field blank or enter your own value.
- **Accept all** — tap **Accept All Suggestions** at the top of the panel to apply every suggested field at once, then review individually as needed.

Once you're satisfied with the fields, tap **Save** to finalize the entry.

<Tip>
  If the AI doesn't recognize a custom or rare firearm, you can still enter all details manually — your entry is saved regardless of whether the AI finds a match. Manually entered values are never overwritten without your action.
</Tip>

## Re-running AI organization

If you add more information to an existing entry — such as correcting the manufacturer name or filling in a previously blank model field — you can ask the AI to re-analyze the entry with the updated data.

To re-run AI organization on an existing entry:

1. Open the firearm's detail view and tap **Edit**.
2. Make your changes to any of the core fields (Name, Model, or Manufacturer).
3. Tap **Re-run AI Analysis** at the bottom of the form.
4. Review the updated suggestions in the **Review AI Suggestions** panel.
5. Accept, edit, or dismiss suggestions as needed, then tap **Save**.

Re-running AI analysis only updates fields where you explicitly accept a new suggestion — existing values you have already confirmed are not overwritten automatically.
