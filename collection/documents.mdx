Keeping your firearms paperwork organized is just as important as keeping your firearms secure. Background check forms, 4473s, and purchase receipts prove lawful acquisition. Warranty cards and service records protect your investment. Having everything in one place makes insurance claims, estate transfers, and resale far less stressful. FireArmory Vault lets you attach any relevant document directly to the firearm it belongs to — encrypted, accessible from anywhere, and always ready when you need it.

<Note>
  All uploaded documents are encrypted before storage using end-to-end encryption. Only you can view or download them. FireArmory Vault staff have no access to your document contents.
</Note>

## Supported document types

FireArmory Vault accepts the following file formats for document uploads:

- **PDF** — ideal for forms, receipts, and multi-page records
- **JPG / JPEG** — for photographed documents or scanned images
- **PNG** — for screenshots or high-resolution scanned images

The maximum file size per upload is **25 MB**. If your document exceeds this limit, consider compressing the PDF or reducing the image resolution before uploading. There is no limit on the number of documents you can attach to a single firearm entry.

## Uploading a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the firearm entry">
    Tap the firearm on your collection dashboard to open its detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the 'Documents' tab">
    In the firearm's detail view, tap the **Documents** tab. Any previously uploaded documents are listed here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Upload'">
    Tap the **Upload** button. Your device's file picker opens — navigate to the document you want to attach and select it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a label">
    Enter a descriptive label for the document (for example, *Background Check — 2023* or *Purchase Receipt — Bass Pro*). A clear label makes it easy to identify the file later without opening it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save**. FireArmory Vault encrypts and uploads the document. It appears in the Documents tab once the upload completes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Organizing documents

Each document attached to a firearm entry is listed in the **Documents** tab with its label, file type, upload date, and file size.

- **View a document** — tap the document label to open it in the in-app viewer. PDFs and images both render inline.
- **Download a document** — tap the **Download** icon next to any document to save a copy to your device.
- **Rename a label** — tap the **⋯** menu next to a document and select **Rename** to update its label.
- **Delete a document** — tap the **⋯** menu and select **Delete**. Confirm when prompted. Deleted documents are permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

Keep labels consistent across your collection to make browsing faster — for example, always label background checks as *Background Check — [Year]* and receipts as *Purchase Receipt — [Retailer]*.

## Document types to store

  <Card title="Background Checks" icon="shield-check">
    Store the results or confirmation of your NICS background check for each firearm purchase.
  </Card>
  <Card title="ATF Form 4473" icon="file-contract">
    Attach completed Firearms Transaction Records for any federally licensed dealer purchases.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Purchase Receipts" icon="receipt">
    Keep itemized receipts from dealers, auction houses, or private sales for cost-basis and resale records.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Warranty Cards" icon="file-certificate">
    Store manufacturer warranty documentation alongside the firearm it covers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Service Records" icon="wrench">
    Log gunsmith work orders, inspection reports, and maintenance records to track the firearm's service history.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Appraisals" icon="magnifying-glass-dollar">
    Attach professional appraisal documents to support insurance valuations or estate planning.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Firearm Schematics" icon="diagram-project">
    Store exploded-view diagrams and parts schematics for each firearm — useful for maintenance and sourcing replacement components.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Building Blueprints" icon="building">
    Attach safe room or storage facility blueprints to document where your firearms are secured at home or at an off-site location.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Deleting a firearm entry permanently removes all documents attached to it. Before you delete any entry, open the Documents tab and download every file you want to keep. Deleted documents cannot be recovered.
</Warning>
