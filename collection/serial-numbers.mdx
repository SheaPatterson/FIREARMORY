Accurate records are the foundation of responsible firearms ownership. Tracking serial numbers protects you in the event of theft or loss, purchase records simplify tax and estate planning, and up-to-date estimated values give your insurance policy something solid to stand on. FireArmory Vault keeps all of this information in one encrypted, secure place — accessible to you and no one else.

<Warning>
  Never share your serial number records publicly. All serial number data in FireArmory Vault is end-to-end encrypted and visible only to you. FireArmory Vault staff cannot access your serial numbers or any other encrypted fields.
</Warning>

## Adding a serial number

Serial numbers are stored in a dedicated encrypted field on each firearm entry. To add or update a serial number:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the firearm entry">
    Tap the firearm on your collection dashboard to open its detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Edit'">
    Tap the **Edit** button in the top-right corner of the detail view to open the entry form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the serial number">
    Locate the **Serial Number** field and type the serial number exactly as it appears on the firearm. The field accepts letters, numbers, and hyphens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the entry">
    Tap **Save**. The serial number is encrypted immediately upon saving and is never stored in plain text.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The serial number field is encrypted at rest using end-to-end encryption. It is masked in the detail view by default — tap the eye icon next to the field to reveal the value when you need to reference it.

## Purchase date and price

Recording when and what you paid for each firearm creates a clear ownership timeline and supports insurance claims or resale negotiations. To add purchase information:

1. Open the firearm entry and tap **Edit**.
2. In the **Purchase Details** section, enter the **Purchase Date** using the date picker.
3. Enter the **Purchase Price** in the currency field. The Vault stores this as the original cost basis for the firearm.
4. Tap **Save** to record the information.

Purchase date and price are stored as part of the firearm's permanent record and are included in collection-wide financial summaries.

## Estimated value tracking

The purchase price captures what you paid — estimated value tracks what the firearm is worth today. FireArmory Vault stores a running history of every estimated value you enter, so you can see how a firearm's value has changed over time.

To add or update an estimated value:

1. Open the firearm entry and tap **Edit**.
2. In the **Estimated Value** field, enter the current market value.
3. Tap **Save**. FireArmory Vault records the new value with a timestamp and adds it to the firearm's value history.

To view the value history for a specific firearm, open its detail view and tap **Value History** in the financial section. Each historical entry shows the recorded value and the date it was entered.

<Info>
  Keeping accurate estimated values is useful for insurance purposes. Consider reviewing and updating your values annually — or any time you make a significant purchase, modification, or sale — to ensure your records reflect current market conditions.
</Info>

## Viewing your records

FireArmory Vault provides a collection-wide financial summary that aggregates data across all your entries. To access it:

1. Open the main menu and tap **Collection Summary**.
2. Select the **Financial Overview** tab.

The Financial Overview displays:

- **Total purchase cost** — the sum of all recorded purchase prices
- **Total estimated value** — the sum of all current estimated values
- **Value change** — the difference between total cost and total estimated value, shown as a dollar amount and percentage
- **Per-firearm breakdown** — a sortable table listing each firearm with its purchase price, estimated value, and last-updated date

Export the Financial Overview as a PDF by tapping **Export** in the top-right corner — useful for sharing with an insurance agent or estate planner.
