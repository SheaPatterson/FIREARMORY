Your collection is the heart of FireArmory Vault. Every firearm you own lives here — complete with serial numbers, purchase records, supporting documents, and AI-generated organization that keeps everything accurate and easy to find. Whether you're managing a handful of heirlooms or a large working inventory, the Vault gives you a single, secure place to track it all.

## Your collection dashboard

When you open FireArmory Vault, your collection dashboard is the first thing you see. The dashboard displays every firearm entry in either a **list view** or a **grid view** — switch between them using the toggle in the top-right corner. Each entry shows the firearm's name, model, manufacturer, and a thumbnail if you've attached an image.

At the top of the dashboard you'll find:

- **Search bar** — instantly filter entries as you type
- **Manufacturer filter** — narrow your view to a single maker
- **Model filter** — drill down to a specific product line
- **Caliber filter** — group firearms by cartridge family
- **Sort controls** — order entries by name, date added, or estimated value

Tap any entry to open its full detail view, where you can review and edit all fields, manage documents, and see AI-generated metadata.

## Searching and filtering

The search bar on your dashboard performs a full-text search across firearm names, models, and manufacturer names. Start typing and the list updates in real time — no need to press Enter.

For more focused browsing, use the filter panel to narrow your results:

- **By manufacturer** — select one or more manufacturers from the dropdown to show only their firearms
- **By model** — filter to a specific product line within a manufacturer
- **By caliber** — group firearms that share a cartridge family (e.g., 9mm, .308 Win, .22 LR)
- **By purchase date range** — set a start and end date to find firearms acquired during a specific period

Combine multiple filters at the same time to isolate exactly the entries you need. Clear all filters with the **Reset** button in the filter panel.

<Tip>
  Use the AI tagging feature to automatically populate manufacturer details — it saves significant time when adding large collections and ensures consistent naming across all your entries.
</Tip>
