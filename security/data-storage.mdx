FireArmory Vault stores your collection data in the cloud so you can access your vault from any device, at any time. Every file, record, and document is encrypted before it leaves your device, so what lives on FireArmory Vault's servers is always ciphertext — unreadable without your password. This page explains exactly where your data goes, how long it stays there, and how you remain in full control of it.

## Where your data is stored

Your encrypted collection data is stored on cloud infrastructure hosted in the United States. All data at rest is protected with AES-256 encryption at the storage layer, on top of the end-to-end encryption applied on your device. Uploaded files — such as scanned background checks, receipts, and schematics — are stored in isolated, access-controlled object storage with the same encryption guarantees as your collection records.

FireArmory Vault does not sell, share, or transfer your personal data or collection records to third parties.

## Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active:

- **Active accounts:** All collection records, documents, and settings are retained indefinitely while your subscription is active or your free account remains open.
- **Deleted entries:** When you delete a firearm entry or document from within the app, it is permanently and immediately removed from FireArmory Vault's systems. Deleted entries are not recoverable.
- **Account deletion:** Deleting your account triggers a permanent purge of all associated data, including collection records, uploaded files, and account metadata. See [Deleting your account](#deleting-your-account) below.

## Exporting your data

You can request a full export of your vault at any time. FireArmory Vault will package your collection records, document metadata, and caliber bookmarks into a downloadable archive and send a link to your registered email address.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the FireArmory Vault app, tap the **Settings** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Privacy">
    Tap **Privacy** in the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Export Data">
    Tap **Export Data** to open the export request screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select data types">
    Choose the data types you want to include in your export — collection records, uploaded documents, caliber bookmarks, or all of the above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request your export">
    Tap **Request Export**. FireArmory Vault will begin preparing your archive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your archive">
    You will receive an email at your registered address containing a secure download link. The link expires after 48 hours, so download your archive promptly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Export your data periodically as a personal backup. The export includes all
  collection records, document metadata, and caliber bookmarks, and can be
  re-imported if you ever need to restore your vault.
</Tip>

## Deleting your account

Deleting your FireArmory Vault account permanently removes all of your data from FireArmory Vault's systems, including every firearm entry, uploaded document, and account record.

<Warning>
  Account deletion is **irreversible**. Once you confirm deletion, your
  collection records, uploaded files, and all account data are permanently
  purged and cannot be recovered — even by FireArmory Vault staff. Export your
  data before proceeding if you want a local copy.
</Warning>

To permanently delete your account:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap **Settings** in the FireArmory Vault app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Privacy">
    Tap **Privacy** in the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Delete Account">
    Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your identity">
    Enter your password to confirm your identity and authorize the deletion request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Tap **Permanently Delete My Account** to finalize. Your data will be purged immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Backups

FireArmory Vault maintains encrypted infrastructure backups for disaster recovery purposes. These backups are used only to restore service availability in the event of a catastrophic system failure — they are not used to restore individual user data.

Because all data stored in FireArmory Vault is end-to-end encrypted, these backups are inaccessible to FireArmory Vault staff. Even in a backup restoration scenario, staff cannot read the contents of your vault. Your privacy guarantees hold at every level of the infrastructure.
