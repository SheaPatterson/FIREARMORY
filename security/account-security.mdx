Your FireArmory Vault account is the gateway to sensitive information — serial numbers, purchase records, legal documents, and a complete inventory of your firearms collection. Protecting that account is not just good practice; it is a responsibility. This page walks you through every security control FireArmory Vault provides and how to use each one effectively.

## Password requirements

FireArmory Vault enforces a minimum password standard to protect your account:

- At least **12 characters** in length
- At least one **uppercase letter**
- At least one **lowercase letter**
- At least one **number**
- At least one **special character** (e.g., `!`, `@`, `#`, `$`)

Meeting the minimum is a floor, not a goal. Because your vault contains uniquely sensitive data, use a long, randomly generated passphrase or password. A **password manager** is the most reliable way to generate and store a strong, unique password for FireArmory Vault — never reuse a password from another service.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication adds a second layer of verification at sign-in, ensuring that a stolen password alone is not enough to access your vault. Enable 2FA as soon as you create your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the FireArmory Vault app, tap the **Settings** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Security">
    Tap **Security** in the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Two-Factor Authentication">
    Tap **Two-Factor Authentication** to open the 2FA setup screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your method">
    Select either **Authenticator App** (recommended) or **SMS**. An authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password generates codes offline and is significantly more resistant to SIM-swapping attacks than SMS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    If you chose an authenticator app, open your app, add a new account, and scan the QR code displayed on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the verification code">
    Type the six-digit code from your authenticator app (or the SMS code) into the verification field in FireArmory Vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your 2FA settings">
    Tap **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** to confirm. 2FA is now active on your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Always use an authenticator app rather than SMS. SMS-based 2FA is vulnerable
  to SIM-swapping attacks; an authenticator app stores your codes locally and
  works even without a cellular signal.
</Tip>

## Recovery codes

Recovery codes are single-use backup codes that let you access your account if you ever lose your 2FA device. Each code can be used only once, after which it is invalidated.

Generate your recovery codes immediately after enabling 2FA:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap **Settings** in the FireArmory Vault app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Security">
    Tap **Security** in the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Recovery Codes">
    Tap **Recovery Codes**. FireArmory Vault will generate a set of one-time-use codes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your codes">
    Copy or download your recovery codes immediately. FireArmory Vault will not show them again after you leave this screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you lose access to your 2FA device and do not have recovery codes, you
  will need to complete identity verification to regain access to your account.
  This process can take **several business days** and may require documentation.
  Do not skip this step — store your recovery codes as soon as you generate
  them.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Print your recovery codes and store them alongside your other important
  documents in a secure physical location, such as a safe or lockbox. A digital
  copy in your password manager is also a good secondary backup.
</Tip>

## Managing active sessions

FireArmory Vault lets you see every device and session currently signed in to your account. Review this list regularly and revoke any session you do not recognize.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap **Settings** in the FireArmory Vault app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Security">
    Tap **Security** in the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Active Sessions">
    Tap **Active Sessions** to see a list of all signed-in devices, including device type, approximate location, and last activity time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revoke a session">
    Tap any session and select **Revoke Access** to sign that device out immediately. To sign out of all devices at once, tap **Revoke All Sessions**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recognizing suspicious activity

FireArmory Vault sends an email notification whenever a new device signs in to your account. If you receive a notification for a sign-in you did not initiate, act immediately:

1. **Change your password** — go to **Settings → Security → Change Password** and set a new, strong password. This invalidates all existing sessions.
2. **Revoke all active sessions** — go to **Settings → Security → Active Sessions** and tap **Revoke All Sessions** to force every device to sign out.
3. **Review your recovery codes** — if your codes may have been exposed, generate a new set from **Settings → Security → Recovery Codes**. Existing codes are invalidated when you regenerate.
4. **Contact support** — reach out to the FireArmory Vault support team at [app.firearmoryvault.com](https://app.firearmoryvault.com) to report the incident. Support can flag your account for additional monitoring and assist with a full security review.

Treat any unrecognized sign-in as a serious event. Because your vault contains firearms inventory data, unauthorized access has real-world implications beyond a typical account breach.
