By the end of this guide, you'll have a fully configured FireArmory Vault account, your first firearm entered and organized by the AI engine, a document attached to that entry, and a working familiarity with the Caliber Wiki. The whole process takes about five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Navigate to [app.firearmoryvault.com](https://app.firearmoryvault.com) and tap **Sign Up**. Enter your email address and choose a strong password, then tap **Create Account**.

    FireArmory Vault sends a verification email to the address you provided. Open that email and tap **Verify Email** to activate your account. You must complete this step before you can upload documents or schematics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your profile">
    After signing in for the first time, FireArmory Vault prompts you to complete your profile. Enter a **display name**. This appears throughout the app and on any exported reports.

    We strongly recommend enabling **two-factor authentication (2FA)** before you add any sensitive data. Navigate to **Settings → Security** and follow the on-screen steps to link an authenticator app. See the [Account Setup guide](/account-setup) for full 2FA instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first firearm">
    From the **Collection** tab, tap **Add Firearm**. Fill in the following fields:

    - **Firearm name** — the common name or product name (e.g., *Model 686*)
    - **Model** — the specific model designation
    - **Manufacturer** — the maker or brand (e.g., *Smith & Wesson*)

    Once you tap **Save**, the AI engine analyzes your input and automatically organizes the entry into the correct category within your collection. No manual sorting is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter key details">
    With the new firearm entry open, tap **Edit Details** and fill in:

    - **Serial number** — stored securely and used for identification
    - **Purchase date** — helps build an accurate ownership timeline
    - **Estimated value** — useful for insurance and resale tracking

    Tap **Save** to commit the changes. All fields are encrypted at rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a document">
    Still inside the firearm entry, tap **Add Document**. Choose a file from your device — a purchase receipt, background check record, or any other relevant paperwork — and tap **Upload**. The document is encrypted automatically and attached directly to this firearm entry for quick retrieval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the Caliber Wiki">
    Tap the **Wiki** tab in the bottom navigation bar. Use the search bar to look up any caliber by name or designation. Each wiki entry displays:

    - Ballistic data including velocity, energy, and trajectory
    - Physical case dimensions and characteristics
    - Case images for visual reference

    Bookmark calibers you reference frequently for faster access later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The AI organization engine works best when you provide complete information. Supply the full firearm name, the exact model designation, and the correct manufacturer name — the more precise your input, the more accurately the AI categorizes and groups your entries.
</Tip>
